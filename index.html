THE EXPERIMENT / 01

What’s quantum here?

No quantum hardware is connected. Your browser simulates three qubits: gates change signed amplitudes, which can reinforce or cancel each other. Each timeline’s weight is its squared amplitude. The no-interference comparison ignores phase between gates. Observe samples from those weights; the next round uses the result to seed a new circuit.

The stories and circuit inputs are generated from local templates, not real-world data. Weights describe this toy model, not the likelihood of actual events. A future backend could run the same circuits on IBM Quantum through Qiskit; that would not make the stories forecasts.
